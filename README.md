## **📱 App Overview**

![unnamed (12)](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/49cd87f4-d3d2-4c6b-aa80-564d89495b82)

**Zeit HRM** is a comprehensive Human Resource Management app designed to simplify and enhance workforce management. It offers robust features for tracking, payroll, recruitment, and employee services while facilitating seamless communication and task management.

---

## **🛠️ Features**

- **Employee Tracking:** Log in and out with location verification.  
- **Payroll Management:** Manage employee payroll effortlessly.  
- **Health Services:** Provide access to health-related features for employees.  
- **Training and Recruitment:** Organize training programs and streamline recruitment processes.  
- **Leave and Holiday Records:** Maintain and manage employee leave and holiday data.  
- **Online Meetings:** Schedule and conduct meetings online.  
- **Push Grievances:** Employees can raise grievances directly through the app.  
- **Task Management:**  
  - Employees can view their assigned tasks.  
  - HR can assign tasks and projects with deadlines.  
- **Set Work Timings:** HR can define and manage employee schedules.  
- **File Sharing:** Share important documents and resources within the team.  
- **Attendance and Performance Tracking:** HR can monitor attendance and evaluate employee performance.  
- **Public Feeds:** HR can post updates or announcements visible to all team members.
